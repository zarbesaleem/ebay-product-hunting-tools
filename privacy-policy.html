Privacy Policy — Ebay Hynter By Wairox Ai

Last updated: 11 October 2026

Ebay Hynter By Wairox Ai ("the extension") is a browser extension that helps you research products on eBay.

What the extension does with data

  • It reads eBay pages that you open (or that the extension opens for your hunt) to find prices, sold counts, titles and result counts, and shows a verdict on the page.
  • Your settings, keyword list, brand edits, hunt state and results are saved only in your own browser (Chrome storage; settings may sync through your own Google account if Chrome Sync is on).

What we do NOT do

  • We do not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data, browsing history, eBay account data or results.
  • We do not run servers for the extension, and use no analytics, tracking or advertising.
  • We do not read your eBay password, payment details or messages.
  • No remote code is loaded; all code is inside the extension package.

Permissions (why they are needed)

  • eBay sites (ebay.com, .co.uk, .de, .com.au, .ca, .fr, .it, .es): to read listing pages and show results.
  • storage: to save your settings and results in your browser.
  • tabs / alarms: to open the search tab for Auto Hunt and pace the searches.
  • downloads: to save your CSV export to your computer.
  • notifications: to tell you when a hunt has finished.

Your control

You can delete all stored data at any time by removing the extension or clearing its data in Chrome.

Children

The extension is not directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above will be updated.
